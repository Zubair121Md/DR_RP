Figure 1. Study design as four independent lanes. The primary lane trains MobileViT-S on APTOS only and selects checkpoints by APTOS validation QWK (n=366); temperature scaling is also fitted on APTOS validation. Evaluation uses APTOS test and three external sets. The CNN and transformer lanes share the APTOS-only training rule; archived EfficientNet-B0 evaluation coverage is shown as stored on disk (sets marked “not evaluated” were not present in the archived metrics files). The LODO lane (dashed) admits non-held-out external images into training and evaluates the held-out external set; it is an ablation of the primary train/external separation. Shared protocol: 224×224 input, ADAMW (lr 1.0e-4, wd 1.0e-4), cosine schedule, 20 epochs.
